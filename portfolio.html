The Art of Silent Narratives

Curated by Prachi Mandal • UGC NET Qualified Visual Arts Researcher, Historian & Curator

The Curatorial Vision

Welcome to a tactile gallery where craft meets the academic depth of art history. Rooted in the natural serenity of Rishikesh, Uttarakhand, this exhibition explores slow, meditative creation between organic elements of the earth and sacred motifs of traditional Indian visual culture.

Drawing from Fine Arts & History of Art at Panjab University, each piece—from large spiritual canvases to translucent skeleton peepal leaf miniatures and hand-molded clay artifacts—hangs from delicate bronze chains, breathing with organic physical life.
